# 🌌 ECHOBOUND — Two Worlds. One Escape.

**Two worlds. One player. Every move changes the puzzle.**

🔗 **Play Now:** https://echoboundworld.vercel.app/

ECHOBOUND is a browser-based puzzle game that challenges players to solve interconnected worlds using echoes, mirrored movement, and strategic planning. Every action has consequences, and reaching the exit requires thinking beyond the world directly in front of you.

Master two unique puzzle modes, manipulate objects, activate mechanisms, and guide both characters to safety before you run out of moves.

**Can you escape both worlds?**

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 1. MIMIC — Every Step Leaves an Echo

<text>Difficulty: Beginner</text>

In MIMIC, your movements create an echo that repeats your actions after a short delay. Your challenge is to plan ahead, manage the timing of your movements, and ensure that both you and your echo can reach your destinations.

**Key mechanics:**
- Move using the arrow keys or WASD.
- Your echo repeats your movements after a delay.
- Press `Space` to wait a turn and let the echo catch up.
- Navigate obstacles, push crates, and activate pressure plates.
- Reach both exits before your available moves run out.

### 2. MIRROR — Two Worlds. Reversed Paths.

<text>Difficulty: Advanced</text>

MIRROR introduces two interconnected worlds where horizontal movement is reversed between the active world and its counterpart.

Moving right in one world makes the shadow move left in the other. Switch between worlds, observe their layouts, and coordinate your movements to solve increasingly challenging spatial puzzles.

**Key mechanics:**
- Control the active world using WASD or the arrow keys.
- Horizontal movements are mirrored in the other world.
- Switch worlds using `Space`, `Tab`, or the on-screen controls.
- Navigate different layouts and coordinate both characters.
- Find a route that leads to both exits.

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 **Dual-World Gameplay:** Solve puzzles across two interconnected environments.
- 👤 **Echo-Based Movement:** Plan around a counterpart that repeats your actions.
- 🪞 **Mirrored Controls:** Master reversed horizontal movement in MIRROR mode.
- 🔄 **World Switching:** Change which world you control to approach puzzles differently.
- 📦 **Interactive Objects:** Push crates to navigate obstacles and manipulate the environment.
- 🔘 **Pressure Plates and Gates:** Activate mechanisms to unlock routes.
- 🔑 **Keys and Doors:** Use puzzle elements to progress through connected environments.
- 🕳️ **Environmental Hazards:** Avoid gaps that can cause a character to fall.
- 🎯 **Dual Exit Objectives:** Guide both characters to their required exits.
- ⏳ **Move-Limited Challenges:** Think carefully before committing to a move.
- ↩️ **Undo and Restart:** Recover from mistakes and experiment with alternative solutions.
- 🏆 **Leaderboard Interface:** Access the leaderboard and player-name interface.
- 🌐 **Play in Your Browser:** Jump into the game without needing a separate game installation.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move | `WASD` or Arrow Keys |
| Switch worlds | `Space` or `Tab` in MIRROR mode |
| Wait a turn | `Space` in MIMIC mode |
| Undo previous move | `Z` |
| Restart level | `R` |
| Pause / Open menu | On-screen Menu button |
| Navigate menus | On-screen buttons |

*Controls may vary by game mode. Follow the in-game instructions for the currently selected level.*

## 🧩 How to Play

1. **Choose your mode:** Start with MIMIC to learn the basics, then progress to MIRROR.
2. **Study both worlds:** Observe the obstacles, exits, and mechanisms before moving.
3. **Plan your route:** Consider how each action affects your character and its counterpart.
4. **Manipulate the environment:** Push crates and activate pressure plates to open gates.
5. **Avoid hazards:** Never step into a gap, and plan the echo's route as carefully as your own.
6. **Manage your moves:** Reach both required exits before the move limit expires.
7. **Learn and retry:** Undo mistakes or restart to discover a better solution.

## 🧠 What Makes ECHOBOUND Different?

Most puzzle games ask you to navigate a single character through a single environment. ECHOBOUND adds another layer: **your actions must account for another character or world with different movement rules.**

Its gameplay revolves around three core ideas:

**Spatial reasoning**

Players must consider positions, obstacles, and routes across interconnected environments.

**Timing and prediction**

In MIMIC, delayed movements require players to think several steps ahead rather than reacting to the current situation.

**Perspective and coordination**

In MIRROR, reversed movement and world switching force players to rethink familiar directional controls.

Together, these mechanics transform simple grid movement into a puzzle of anticipation, experimentation, and precision.
